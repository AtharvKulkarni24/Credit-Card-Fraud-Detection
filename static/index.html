Academic Viva Insight: Why use Anomaly Detection if Supervised RF has higher raw F1?

In real-world payment processing, fraud is an adversarial moving target. Supervised models only detect patterns they have seen in training data, meaning they completely miss zero-day attacks and novel fraud techniques. In contrast, Isolation Forest isolates anomalies purely based on statistical divergence from legitimate transactions ($V_{14}, V_{12}$ drop, unusual spending velocity), requiring no prior fraud labels and protecting the payment network against brand new exploits.
